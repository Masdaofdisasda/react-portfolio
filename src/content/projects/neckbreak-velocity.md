**Neck Break Velocity** is a chaotic, two-player split-screen racing game built in **Unity** using **C#** and the **High-Definition Render Pipeline (HDRP)**. The concept is simple: you're a person running absurdly fast through a crowd of NPCs, racing your opponent to the finish line while dodging oncoming pedestrians.

The twist? Crashes trigger over-the-top **ragdoll physics**, sending characters flying, making every mistake hilarious and memorable.

I developed the game with a friend, handled the **soundtrack and audio design**, and worked on gameplay logic, input handling, and HDRP-based visual effects. The project gave us a great opportunity to explore **Unity's input system**, **split-screen camera design**, and general gameplay polish.

### 🎮 Key Features
- **Two-Player Split Screen**: Couch-coop racing
- **Dynamic Ragdoll Crashes**: Collisions result in physically simulated chaos
- **HDRP Visuals**: High-fidelity rendering with custom lighting & effects
- **Original Soundtrack**: Composed and mixed music and sound FX
- **Character Customization**: Players can personalize their racers

This project was a great mix of fun and technical challenge, and it taught me a lot about game feel, input systems, and Unity’s rendering stack — plus how to deliver a polished, playable game from start to finish.
